# Custom Climbing Route Maker

CodePath WEB103 Final Project

Designed and developed by: Isaiah Linares, Francisco Burgos, Abha Mam, Han Luu, Arbaaz Attar

## About

### Description and Purpose

Custom Climbing Route Maker lets you build your own bouldering routes on a virtual climbing wall. You pick the holds, mark where the route starts and finishes, give it a name and a grade, and save it. Other climbers can browse the routes, filter them by how hard they are, and try them at their own gym.

We made it for two kinds of people: climbers who want new problems to try, and route setters who want a quick way to sketch out an idea before putting real holds on a real wall.

### Inspiration

Isaiah boulders a lot, and at the gym people use training boards like the Tension Board. You pick holds in an app and the wall lights them up so you know what to climb. It's a great idea, but one of those boards is costly. We wanted something free that anyone can open in a browser.

## Features

### Route Builder

Pick holds on a blank wall to build your own route. Click a hold to add it, and click it again to take it off.

### Start, Hand, Foot, and Finish Holds

Every hold on a route gets a job: start, hand, foot, or finish. Each one shows up in a different color, so you can tell at a glance how the route works.

### Route Gallery

See every saved route as a card with its name, grade, and a small picture of the wall.

### Route Page

Every route has its own page and its own link (like /routes/12), so you can open it straight from the gallery or text it to a friend.

### Edit a Route

Change a route's name, grade, or holds and save it again.

### Delete a Route

Get rid of a route you don't want anymore. The app asks "Are you sure?" first so you don't delete something by accident.

### Filter and Sort by Grade

Show only routes at a certain grade (like V3), or line them up from easiest to hardest.

### Quick Look

Click a route card and it pops open in a window on top of the gallery, so you can check it out without leaving the page.

### Save Check

The app won't save a route unless it has a name, a grade, at least one start hold, and a finish hold. If something's missing, it tells you what to fix.

### Reset the Wall

One button that wipes everything and brings back the starter routes. Handy for testing and for demo day.
