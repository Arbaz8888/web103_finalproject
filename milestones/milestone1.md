## Reflection

### 1. What went well during this unit?

We got along right away. In our first meeting everyone introduced themselves, we set up a Slack channel, and we picked an idea that same day. People threw out a bunch of options (an online store, a boba drink maker, a workout tracker), and when Isaiah pitched the climbing route idea, all five of us were into it. We also agreed early to build a simple version first and only add the fancy stuff if we have time left.

### 2. What were some challenges your group faced in this unit?

Picking one idea out of so many took a while. We also went back and forth on how big to make this. Some of us really want a 3D wall and AI features, and others worried that's too much for five weeks. We weren't sure how we'd even store 3D data on Render's plan. Not everyone on the team climbs, so Isaiah had to explain things like grades and what makes a route hard.

### 3. What additional support will you need in upcoming units as you continue to work on your final project?

We'd like help figuring out the best way to save where each hold sits on the wall in our database. If we get to the 3D version, some pointers on using Spline or Three.js with React would help a lot. We'll probably also need help deploying to Render (and figuring out if the free plan is enough).
